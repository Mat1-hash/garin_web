Father Garin's influence on education

Garin College, in Nelson, New Zealand, is a Catholic high school named after Father Antoine Garin.
It opened in 2002 to honor his work in education and community.
Father Garin started St. Mary’s School in the 19th century, laying the groundwork for Catholic education in the area.
Even after his death, he is still teaching us, as Garin College carries on his mission by focusing on well-rounded learning, community values, and the Catholic faith.
