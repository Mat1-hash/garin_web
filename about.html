About Father Garin

Father Antoine Garin (1810-1889) was a French missionary who came to New Zealand in 1841.
He helped build Catholic communities, mainly in the Nelson area, where he started St. Mary’s Church and school.
Garin worked with both Māori and European settlers, learning the Māori language and supporting their needs.
He is remembered for his work in education and bringing people together, helping to grow the Catholic Church in New Zealand.
